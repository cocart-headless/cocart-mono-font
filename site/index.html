About
For storesNumbers you can trust at a glance

Slashed zero, serifed i, l and 1, and open apertures keep codes unambiguous. Tabular figures line totals up in carts, receipts and invoices, and every currency sign up to ₿ is drawn.

For developersA font that does more than draw

Turn on ss02, or install CoCart Mono Masked, and API keys show as dots on screen shares and recordings while the text stays intact. Box drawing, blocks, Braille and agent marks are drawn on the same grid, so terminals and CLIs never fall back to another font.

One skeletonNine weights, one drawing

Every glyph is drawn as a single centre line and weighted last, so Thin and Black share exactly the same skeleton. Figures, codes and grids never shift between weights, and the in-between weights of the variable font are exact, not guessed.

01Principles

Precision

One cell for every glyph, so a narrow 1 takes the same space as a wide 8. Prices stack on the decimal point with no tabular settings.

Clarity

The look-alikes that cause misread codes are drawn apart: 0 and O, 1, l and I, 5 and S, 8 and B. A coupon or SKU reads right the first time.

Function

Currency signs, percent, hash, multiply and arrows were drawn first, not last. They are the symbols a storefront uses on every page.

03Legibility · the look-alikes
Zero is slashed and narrower than O. Lowercase o sits lower and rounder.
1 has a flag, l a curved tail, I serifs top and bottom, i a slab foot. The bar stays plain.
5 turns a hard corner where S and s curve. $ has short ticks above and below, not a bar through it.
3 has a flat top. 8 stacks a small bowl on a larger one, so it never reads as B.
The foot serif on r keeps rn from merging into m.
2 starts with a curve. Z and z are all straight lines.
6 and 9 use straight strokes, not stems, so they never echo b, g or q.
Hyphen, minus, en dash and em dash each have their own length.
04Six scripts, one grid

Cyrillic reuses the Latin skeletons wherever the letters match — А В Е К М Н О Р С Т Х — and the new forms are drawn to the same 600-unit cell. A Russian price list lines up with an English one, character for character.

Lowercase Cyrillic is drawn on its own at the 530 x-height — д and ц drop into the descender, л gets a curved leg — so it reads as a text face, not small capitals. Accents are separate parts placed by rule, so one fix to an acute updates every letter that uses it.

05Italic — a true second voice

The italic leans 9° and swaps in cursive forms where readers expect them. In Latin that's a single-storey a and a descending f. In Cyrillic the change is bigger: и becomes u, п becomes n, т becomes m, г turns into a reversed s and д gets a looped ascender.

Use it for sale notes, product variants and secondary details — the line a shopper reads after the price, not instead of it.

06Family — nine weights, one skeleton

Thin 100 to Black 900, each with an italic. Every weight is drawn from the same centre lines with a thicker pen, from 20 units to 124, so the grid never moves: a Black total sits directly over a Thin line item, and every style keeps the 0.600 em cell. Wide letters (m, w, ж, ш, щ) carry a lighter stroke at every weight, so they stay open at Black.

Thin and ExtraLight for display prices at 40px and up, Light for large prices, Regular for body and line items, Medium and SemiBold for buttons and labels, Bold for totals, ExtraBold and Black for sale badges and hero numbers.

09Keeps a secret

CoCart Mono can draw API keys as dots. Paste a WooCommerce consumer secret during a screen share and the call sees cs_9b8a7c6d5e4f, while the clipboard, the file and your shell history keep every character. The font does it on its own: no plugin, nothing for the app to know.

Turn on ss02 in CoCart Mono, or install CoCart Mono Masked, where it's always on for apps with no font settings. The first four characters after a prefix stay readable so you can tell keys apart. Keep both off the storefront: they hide text from shoppers too.

Contexts are the words around a secret, in any case, including wp-config salts: consumer_secret=, "api_key": ", Bearer , --token . A mask never starts inside a word, public keys stay readable, and a mask ends at the first character a key can't contain. It changes pixels, not text: copy, files, logs and other programs still see the key, and a key that wraps onto a second line shows. If a key has been seen, rotate it.
