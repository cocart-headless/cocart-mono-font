Please check our letters

CoCart Mono is a monospaced typeface for shop prices, order numbers and codes. We drew Thai, Armenian, Georgian, Cyrillic and Greek ourselves, and we need a native reader to tell us where the letters are wrong, unclear or odd-looking. Nothing here needs design knowledge — read it as you would a receipt or a shop page.
